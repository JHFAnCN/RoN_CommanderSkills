Commander Skills (RoN_CommanderSkills)
What This Mod Does
This mod adds a Commander Skills system to Reign of Nether RTS. It is an independent add-on mod and requires Reign of Nether RTS to be installed first.

When playing in RTS mode by selecting a faction, a skill panel will appear on the left side of the screen. Each faction has its own set of skills: they require food, wood, and minerals for unlocking and upgrading, and each skill activation also consumes a usage cost. Unlocking and upgrading also depend on you first constructing specific buildings. Skills have a cooldown period after use.

Villager (Havoc) Faction:

Battle Horn: Applies buffs (increased attack damage, attack speed, and movement speed) to friendly units within a selected area for a duration.

R&R: Creates a healing zone at a selected location, continuously restoring health to friendly units within the area.

Cauldron Rave: Generates an effect circle at a selected location. Enemy/neutral units inside receive random negative effects (such as slowness, weakness), while friendly/allied units can receive buffs.

Monster (Undead) Faction:

The Undying: For a duration, all global friendly/allied undead units regenerate health per second (activates without needing to click on the map).

Sculk Blessing: Within a selected area, friendly units are protected from burning during the day, and undead units have a chance to revive upon death.

Phantom Task Force: Select a circular area; after a delay, Creeper units are summoned from above within that area.

Piglin Faction:

Efficient Portal: For a duration, the training speed of all buildings that produce units is increased (global effect).

Bounty: Marks enemy units within a selected area; when a marked unit dies, you receive resource rewards based on the skill's usage cost.

Displacement Portal: Left-click on one unit, then left-click on another unit (friendly, enemy, or neutral) to swap their positions.

In-game, each skill and upgrade button has a hover tooltip explaining the costs, cooldown, and building requirements. How to use: Left-click to select a skill, then left-click on the map to activate it (or follow the on-screen prompts). Right-click to cancel the current selection.
